# ABNT NBR 14724:2011 - Core Formatting Requirements


### 1. Page Setup (Preparo da Página)

|---------|--------------------------------|------------------------------|--------|
| **Paper size** | A4 (210mm x 297mm) | Usually follows ABNT | ✅ Standard |
| **Font** | Times New Roman 12pt or Arial 12pt | May specify one font | ⚠️ Verify |
| **Line spacing** | 1.5 lines | Some TCCs require 1.5 or double | ⚠️ Verify |
| **Paragraph indent** | 1.25cm first line | Usually follows ABNT | ✅ Standard |
| **Page numbers** | Bottom center, from first page | May differ for pre-textual | ⚠️ Verify |


---

### 2. Pre-Textual Elements (Elementos Pré-Textuais)

|---------|------------------|------------------------------|----------|
| **Title page (Folha de rosto)** | Extended cover with more details | Usually follows ABNT | ABNT |
| **Abstract (Resumo)** | Required in Portuguese | Mandatory | ABNT |
| **Keywords (Palavras-chave)** | 3-5 keywords | Mandatory | ABNT |
| **Table of contents** | Required | Mandatory | ABNT |


---

### 3. Textual Structure (Estrutura Textual)

|---------|----------------|-------------------|-------|
| **Chapter numbering** | Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3...) | Usually follows ABNT | Standard |
| **Section numbering** | 1.1, 1.1.1, 1.1.1.1 | Usually follows ABNT | Standard |
| **Introduction** | First chapter, no number | Usually follows ABNT | Verify |
| **Chapter titles** | ALL CAPS, bold, centered | May vary (bold, uppercase) | Verify font style |
| **Section titles** | Title Case, bold, left-aligned | Verify capitalization | Check manual |
| **Subsections** | Sentence case, bold | Verify format | Check manual |

- Introduction may be numbered as "Chapter 1" instead of unnumbered
- Chapter titles may use Title Case instead of ALL CAPS
- Section numbering may have different depth limits (e.g., max 3 levels)

---

### 4. Post-Textual Elements (Elementos Pós-Textuais)

|---------|------------------|-----------------|--------|

**Important**: ABNT distinguishes between:
- **Appendix**: Author's own supplementary material
- **Annex**: External/secondary supplementary material

---

### 5. Citation and References (Citações e Referências)

|---------|------------------------|------------|----------|
| **Citation format** | (Author, Year) or Author (Year) | Usually follows ABNT | ABNT |
| **Direct quote** | "Text" (Author, Year, p. xx) | Usually follows ABNT | ABNT |
| **Reference style** | Author-date system | Usually follows ABNT | ABNT |
| **Reference list order** | Alphabetical by author | Usually follows ABNT | ABNT |


---

### 6. Tables and Figures (Quadros, Tabelas e Figuras)

|---------|------------------|---------------|-------|
| **Caption position** | Top for tables; Bottom for figures | May specify differently | ⚠️ |
| **Numbering** | Continuous or per chapter (1, 2, 3 or 1.1, 1.2) | Usually follows ABNT | ✅ |
| **Source note** | Mandatory for all tables/figures | Mandatory | ABNT |
| **Title** | Above table; Below figure | Verify position | ⚠️ |
| **Frame** | Tables have frames; Figures may not | Verify | ⚠️ |

**ABNT Terminology**:
- **Quadro**: Textual data in framed format
- **Tabela**: Numerical data
- **Figura**: Graphs, charts, maps, images
