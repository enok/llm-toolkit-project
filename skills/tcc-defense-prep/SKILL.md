# TCC Defense Preparation (USP MBA)

Systematic preparation for thesis defense (banca).

> **Reference**: See USP MBA TCC manual in `../../tcc/Manual de Instruções e Normas TCC_PT.pdf` (section on defesa).

## Quick Reference

### Standard Defense Structure (20 min presentation)
1. **Hook + problem** (2 min) — Why this matters, who cares
2. **Research question + objectives** (2 min) — Crisp question, 2-3 specific objectives
3. **Methodology** (5 min) — Data sources, analytical approach, why this method
4. **Results** (7 min) — Key findings with 3-4 punchy charts/tables
5. **Limitations + future work** (2 min) — Honest boundaries
6. **Conclusion + contribution** (2 min) — What's new, what's useful

**Then**: 20-30 min Q&A from banca (typically 2-3 examiners)

### Slide Design Rules
- **One idea per slide** — title is the takeaway, body is the evidence
- **Chart > table > bullet list** — show data visually
- **Font size ≥ 24pt** — examiners may be far from screen
- **No walls of text** — if you're reading slides, audience is reading them too
- **Backup slides** (after "Thank you") — raw tables, robustness checks, extra figures

### Likely Examiner Questions (prepare answers)
Prioritize by likelihood:

1. **"Why this method and not [alternative]?"** → Know 2-3 alternatives and their tradeoffs
2. **"How did you handle [confounder]?"** → Have explicit answer for each major confounder
3. **"What about causality vs correlation?"** → Be explicit about limits; don't overclaim
4. **"Data quality issues?"** → Show you know them (coverage gaps, measurement error)
5. **"External validity?"** → Who/what generalizes, who doesn't
6. **"Practical implications?"** → One-sentence actionable takeaway per stakeholder
7. **"Why didn't you also [X]?"** → Scope was bounded; acknowledge and suggest for future work
8. **"How does this extend [literature]?"** → Know 2-3 papers intimately

### Domain-Specific Q's (for federal transfers / compliance thesis)
- Endogeneity — why transfers and compliance may co-determine
- LGPD compliance — what data you could/couldn't use
- Policy recommendations — are they realistic under Brazilian institutional constraints?
- Sanction registries as outcome — known biases (detection vs actual compliance)

### Timing Discipline
- **Rehearse 3+ times** at full pace — measure actual time, not planned
- **Cut 10% from planned content** — you will be slower under pressure
- **Have slides/content for 15-min, 20-min, and 25-min versions** — banca may compress

### Handling Tough Moments
- **"I don't know, but here's how I'd find out"** is acceptable
- **"That's outside the scope I defined because [reason]"** for out-of-scope challenges
- **Never argue defensively** — acknowledge valid point, then add your angle
- **Take notes** during Q&A — shows respect, helps with revisions

## When to Apply

- 2-4 weeks before defense date
- After receiving preliminary committee feedback
- When practicing presentation out loud
- When designing slide deck structure
- When anticipating tough questions

## Post-Defense Steps

1. Submit revised version within committee-set deadline (usually 15-30 days)
2. Apply committee revisions explicitly (tracked changes help)
3. Get final approval signature sheet
4. Submit final PDF to USP digital repository
