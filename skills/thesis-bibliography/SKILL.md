# Thesis Bibliography and References Skill

Use this skill to justify all thesis decisions, methods, and frameworks with proper academic references. Maps each knowledge area to essential bibliographical sources.

## Purpose

Every analytical decision, method choice, and framework application in the thesis must be supported by bibliographical references. This skill provides:
- Required references for each knowledge area
- Citation format (ABNT)
- When to cite each reference
- Portuguese and English sources

---

## 1. Research Methodology (Metodologia de Pesquisa)

### Core References (Mandatory)

| Reference | Citation (ABNT) | When to Use |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Gil (2010)** | GIL, A. C. **Como elaborar projetos de pesquisa**. 5. ed. São Paulo: Atlas, 2010. | Research design, project structure, scientific method justification |
| **Lakatos & Marconi (2010)** | LAKATOS, E. M.; MARCONI, M. A. **Fundamentos de metodologia científica**. 7. ed. São Paulo: Atlas, 2010. | Scientific methodology, hypothesis formulation, research phases |
| **Cervo & Bervian (2002)** | CERVO, A. L.; BERVIAN, P. A. **Metodologia científica**. 6. ed. São Paulo: Pearson, 2002. | Research problem definition, literature review methodology |
| **Hernández et al. (2010)** | HERNÁNDEZ, R.; FERNÁNDEZ, C.; BAPTISTA, P. **Metodologia da pesquisa científica**. Porto Alegre: Artmed, 2010. | Complete research methodology framework |
| **Projeto de Pesquisa Esalq** | ESALQ/USP. **Template Projeto de Pesquisa MBA Data Science**. São Paulo, 2024. | Project structure, chapter organization, deliverables |

### Application in Thesis
- **Introduction chapter**: Cite Gil (2010) and Lakatos & Marconi (2010) for research problem formulation
- **Methodology chapter**: Cite Cervo & Bervian (2002) for methodological choices
- **Justification**: Reference Hernández et al. (2010) for research phase organization

## Citation Templates (ABNT Format)

### Book
```
AUTHOR, A. A. **Title of the book in bold**. Edition (if not 1st). City: Publisher, Year.
```

### Book Chapter
```
AUTHOR, A. A. Title of the chapter. In: EDITOR, E. E. (Org.). **Title of the book in bold**. City: Publisher, Year. p. xx-xx.
```

### Journal Article
```
AUTHOR, A. A. Title of the article. **Journal Name in bold**, v. xx, n. x, p. xx-xx, Year.
```

### Online Resource
```
AUTHOR, A. A. Title of the work. **Website Name**, Year. Available at: <URL>. Accessed on: Day Month Year.
```

### Technical Report/Whitepaper
```
ORGANIZATION. **Title of the report in bold**. City: Organization, Year.
```
