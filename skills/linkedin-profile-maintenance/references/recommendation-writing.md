# Recommendation writing and re-requesting

Two jobs touch the Recommendations section. Both are external writes in the
owner's name, so every message is a draft the owner approves
(`rules/external-write-authorization.md`).

## A. Writing a recommendation for another person

1. **Centre the subject, not the author.** The text answers "what is this
   person like to work with", so lead with their contribution.
2. **Read peers' recommendations first.** Open the subject's existing
   recommendations (or ask the owner for examples) and match their tone and
   what they emphasise; a recommendation that reads differently from the
   others stands out.
3. **Use only facts the owner supplies**: who started what, which meetings or
   projects happened. If the owner's facts contradict the draft's credit
   claims, fix the draft, not the facts.
4. **Deliver one final text** the owner can paste, shown in full in its own
   message, with questions in a separate turn.

## B. Re-requesting recommendations in another language

1. **Find the owner's earlier wording** for the same request in past chats or
   mail and reuse it; do not invent a new voice.
2. **Include the exact proposed text** in the request, so the recommender only
   has to confirm or edit it.
3. **Per-recipient drafts go to the owner; nothing is sent without approval.**
4. **Skip people who already have one in the target language.**
5. **Platform behaviour (observed 2026-11, may change):** the request form
   errors with "already recommended for this position", so the recommender has
   to write a new one, and the old-language entry stays visible beside the new
   one. Tell the owner the old entry cannot be replaced from the request flow.
