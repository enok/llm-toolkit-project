# Plan deliverable format and re-planning

Read when producing or revising the plan document the candidate will actually
use day to day, and whenever the candidate reports progress. These rules come
from corrections candidates made to earlier plans; each one removes a reason
the plan stops being used.

## Deliverable rules

1. **One table of all links in timeline order**, each item with its total
   duration, checked against the slots the candidate really has. A plan whose
   items do not fit the slots is wishful, not a plan.
2. **Video first when the candidate is tired** (evenings, after work); reading
   and drills go to the fresh slots.
3. **Real checkboxes** (interactive in a sheet or document, or printable
   squares in a PDF), not glyphs typed into text, so progress can be saved.
4. **Same content in the file, not only in chat.** The file is the source of
   truth; chat summarises it.
5. **Done items are pre-ticked and removed from the schedule** (for example
   course sections already finished), so time is not double-booked.
6. **Weekday-only schedule (Monday to Friday)** unless the candidate asks for
   weekends, with the total time reported at the end.
7. **One tab per week**, copied from an untouched template tab (the template is
   never edited, so the next week starts clean).
8. **Purchase list** with a column own / free / buy / drop-from-cart; nothing
   is bought on the candidate's behalf.
9. **Mentorship or review sessions come last** in the timeline order, after the
   preparation they depend on.
10. **Books**: recommend only from the candidate's real tracker and goals, each
    with a verified source (publisher or library page), never invented. For a
    language the candidate has chosen, ask for ONE canonical book and map its
    chapters to videos instead of listing several.

## Re-planning from a progress report

When the candidate says "I did until item 8" or "starting earlier tomorrow":

1. Tick items 1-8, remove them from the schedule, recompute remaining hours.
2. Re-flow the remaining items into the new slots, keeping the order of
   dependencies; report what moved and what no longer fits.
3. Replace items that are too thin (a title with no substance) with video or
   post links, each verified.
4. **Verify every link opens in the candidate's own browser** (a course deep
   link can fail in one browser and work in another) and offer the working
   variant.
5. End with the updated total time and the next three items.
