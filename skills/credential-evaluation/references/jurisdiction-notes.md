# Jurisdiction notes

Starting points for step 3 of `SKILL.md` ("recognition where it will be used").
Every dated item is perishable: re-open the source before relying on it, and
send binding questions to the regulator or the receiving institution. These
notes are not legal advice.

For any other country, ask the same four questions: who recognizes foreign
degrees, what filters they apply (delivery mode, accreditation, level), what
it costs and how long it takes, and what the degree can legally be used for
without recognition.

## Brazil (verified 2026-10-21 — re-verify before relying)

Foreign master's or doctorate (stricto sensu):

- It has no stricto sensu validity until a Brazilian university that runs a
  recognized program in the same or an equivalent area grants
  "reconhecimento" (LDB art. 48 par. 3; Resolução CNE/CES 3/2016, amended by
  Resolução CNE/CES 1/2022). Summary of the 2022 amendment from a law-firm
  explainer (grade B): https://www.mattosfilho.com.br/unico/cne-diplonas-estrangeiros/
- Requests go through Plataforma Carolina Bori, which is mandatory. A
  simplified track exists. Standard deadline 180 days, simplified 90 days;
  in practice often longer.
- Portaria MEC 1.151/2023 covers graduação (undergraduate) only, not
  master's or doctorates.
- Each university sets its own filters, and distance delivery is the common
  trap:
  - UFMG Edital 579/2025 does not accept distance, hybrid, or remote foreign
    master's or doctorates. Example fee: R$ 2,016.62 (2025).
    https://www.ufmg.br/prpg/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/SEI_4018623_Edital_579.pdf
  - UFRJ Resolução CEPG 395/2025 does not accept "cursos a distância ou
    semipresenciais". https://pesquisa.ufrj.br/pdfs/resolucaoCEPG2025_395
  - UnB treats modality as an equivalence parameter, not an exclusion.
    https://dpg.unb.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Resolucao_do_CEPE_n__0095_2025.pdf

Without reconhecimento:

- Private employers may value the title freely.
- It does not count as "mestrado" for public-sector competitions (concursos),
  career progression, or university teaching. A 2015 federal regional court
  (TRF3) decision supports requiring validation of foreign postgraduate
  titles:
  https://web.trf3.jus.br/noticias/Noticiar/ExibirNoticia/330030-titulo-de-posgraduacao-no-exterior-deve-ser-validado

Domestic comparison:

- A Brazilian MBA or especialização is lato sensu (at least 360 hours,
  certificate with national validity). It is not a mestrado.
- A recognized foreign MSc would outrank it legally; an unrecognized one has
  no formal standing, so for legal weight it ranks below the domestic
  certificate.

Checks: read the target university's current edital or resolution for the
delivery-mode filter before enrolling; confirm the program area has a
recognized domestic program; budget for fee and months of waiting.

## United States (verified 2026-10-21 — re-verify before relying)

- Institutional accreditation comes from accreditors recognized by USDE or
  CHEA. State authorization or approval to operate is not accreditation.
- A foreign degree is usually judged through a credential evaluation by a
  NACES member (for example WES or ECE). Some evaluators do not evaluate
  certain foreign institutions, so ask the evaluator before enrolling.
- The H-1B advanced-degree (master's) cap requires a degree from a US
  institution of higher education (public or nonprofit, accredited). Source is
  a law-firm blog (grade B):
  https://rnlawgroup.com/838-reminder-ensure-registrants-qualify-for-the-h-1b-master-s-cap-2
  Re-verify on uscis.gov before relying.

## European Union (verified 2026-10-21 — re-verify before relying)

- EQF level 7 is master's level. 60 ECTS is about one full-time year, at
  25-30 hours of work per ECTS.
- The Lisbon Recognition Convention governs recognition among its parties but
  recognition is not automatic: each country's recognition body decides.
- Check that the institution's national QA agency is registered on EQAR. In
  2025 the EQAR Register Committee rejected Malta's MFHEA application:
  https://www.eqar.eu/eqar-statement-on-the-outcome-of-mfheas-application/
- Regulators publish institution-specific notices on their own announcements
  pages; read the one for the awarding body, for example
  https://mfhea.mt/resources/announcements/
- Germany's EU Blue Card for IT specialists accepts a recognized degree or at
  least 3 years of IT experience in the last 7 years (provider blog, grade B;
  re-verify on make-it-in-germany.com).
